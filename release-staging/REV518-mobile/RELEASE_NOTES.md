New Perbandingan Hari Profit & Loss chart sits directly under Tracking PnL Harian. It pairs green profit-day and red loss-day counts for Monday through Sunday, with numeric labels, gradients, a shared count axis, day filters, overlays and trade details. One Closed At GMT +7 date counts once using its aggregate net PnL in the calendar's selected Pips/Dollar mode; individual trades are not counted as separate days. Net-zero/no-trade days do not add profit/loss counts. Days with incomplete Dollar pricing are reported separately.

Calendar weekday headers repeat under every year, retaining six Monday-first week blocks and red Monday headers. Desktop month labels retain their full-width column. Mobile uses a seven-column header per year, without horizontal scrolling.

Follows the selected analyst, single pair, year, raw full-trade data and shared cost settings. All previous Closed At, weighted R, two-decimal display, ten-row summaries and unload-warning fixes remain.

Validated with mixed profit/loss trades on one date, net-zero days, Dollar fee sign changes, unknown pricing, multiple years, day filters and responsive layouts from 280 to 1440px.
