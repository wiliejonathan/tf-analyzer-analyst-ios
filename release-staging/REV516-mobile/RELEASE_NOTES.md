REV516 / v1.18.29

Tracking PnL Harian now aligns every month to Monday-first week columns. Desktop shows one month per row with six fixed weeks and repeating M, T, W, T, F, S, S headers. Monday headers are red; the former violet cell outlines/legend are removed. Empty cells precede day 1 and follow the last date; these are distinct from valid dates with no transactions. Each valid cell shows its date number, with existing profit/loss/net-zero fills, Pips/Dollar tooltip and trade details retained.

On phones/tablets the same aligned slots wrap into seven-column month calendars without horizontal scrolling or an inner vertical scrollbar. Verified across all seven possible starting weekdays, 36 consecutive months, leap-year February and 280–1440px viewports. Report calculations and shared adjustment controls are unchanged. The plugin retains REV515's unload-policy warning fix.
